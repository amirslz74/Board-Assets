# MOSFET Selection Guide
Quick reference for common MOSFETs used in embedded, automotive, and industrial PCB designs.

---

## Logic-Level MOSFETs
> Gate threshold ~1-2V — driven directly by STM32 / ESP32 / Arduino without a gate driver IC.

| Part | Vds | Id | Rds(on) | Package | Best For |
|------|-----|----|---------|---------|----------|
| IRL540N | 100V | 36A | 44mΩ | TO-220 | General switching, motor control |
| IRLZ44N | 55V | 47A | 28mΩ | TO-220 | High current motor/load switching |
| IRL3803 | 30V | 140A | 6mΩ | TO-220 | Very high current, low voltage |
| BSS138 | 50V | 200mA | 3.5Ω | SOT-23 | Level shifting, small signal |
| 2N7000 | 60V | 200mA | 5Ω | TO-92 | Signal switching, low side |

---

## Standard MOSFETs
> Requires 10V+ gate drive — use with IR2104 / IR2110 or similar gate driver IC.

| Part | Vds | Id | Rds(on) | Package | Best For |
|------|-----|----|---------|---------|----------|
| IRFZ44N | 55V | 49A | 17.5mΩ | TO-220 | Most popular general purpose |
| IRF3205 | 55V | 110A | 8mΩ | TO-220 | High current, H-bridge, inverter |
| IRF540N | 100V | 33A | 77mΩ | TO-220 | General switching up to 100V |
| IRF9540N | 100V | 23A | 117mΩ | TO-220 | P-channel, high-side switch |
| IRF4905 | 55V | 74A | 20mΩ | TO-220 | P-channel high current |

---

---

## Quick Selection Guide

```
Need to switch from STM32/ESP32 pin directly?
  → Logic-Level: IRL540N / IRLZ44N / BSS138

Need high current motor control with gate driver?
  → Standard: IRF3205 / IRFZ44N

Building an automotive ECU or car electronics?
  → Automotive: STP55NF06 / IPB025N10N

Building a power supply or industrial inverter?
  → High Voltage: IRF840 / IPW60R045

Need P-channel for high-side switching?
  → IRF9540N (standard) / IRF4905 (high current)
```
